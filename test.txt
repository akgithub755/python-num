Summary
core_ori.py is the domain model layer of this pipeline. It contains:
ClassRoleDataReads CSV + reference files, splits into typed listsAchatModels a purchase, parses survey data, inserts to DBPanierModels a basket, validates dates/shops, inserts to DBPollingModels a product polling record, decodes CIVI/QIRI characteristics
The key flow is: Data splits the CSV → Panier and Achat objects are created → Achat objects are linked to Panier via UpdateFromPanier → Polling objects are created from Achat → everything is validated and inserted into the database.
